---
name: Approved public contact channels
description: Rule for public contact details on this hospital website.
---

Only publish phone numbers and email addresses approved by the site owner. Do not reuse unverified contact text from older photos or legacy copy; consult the current site configuration for approved values.

**Why:** The site owner explicitly restricted which public phone numbers and email may appear.

**How to apply:** Verify new templates, content, seed data, links, and assets against the current configured channels before publishing.
